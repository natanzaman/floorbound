# Floorbound: story bible

**Season 1: "The First Bell"**: three short episodes (2–4 min each) acted live by bots in the game and filmed.
Original characters and places. The tone of SAO (trapped, death is real, raid parties, a sacrifice, a clutch Switch), none of its story.

Files:
- `docs/story/STORY.md`: this bible (world, cast, arc, episode beats).

- `docs/story/ep2-mirelight.md`, `ep3-second-bell.md`: Episodes 2 and 3 screenplays (cue sheets come in ROADMAP F7).
- `docs/SCRIPT_BRIEF.md`: what the game and the director can do (the capability list writers work from).

## The world

**The tower.** Two thousand players logged in on launch night. The logout button was gone by morning. Carved on the back of the Hall of Clearers, where nobody remembers it being carved:

> *A HUNDRED BELLS. THEN HOME.*

**The rules, as the players understand them**
- **The bells.** When a floor is cleared for the first time, a bell rings through the whole tower, every floor at once. One bell per floor. A hundred bells and the doors open. (In the game: the Tower Bell, see ROADMAP F6.)
- **Death is real.** Health at zero and you're gone, here and outside. The game respawns characters, but the story never shows a respawn: on film, a fallen character just never comes back.
- **The Hall of Clearers.** The only place in the tower where names are written. Every party that beats a floor boss is carved there, the fallen included, if they fought.
- **The day count.** Players count days since the gates closed. On Day 41 the Floor 1 stone is still blank. Three raids tried. Few came back.

**Places used this season**: all already in the game.
| Place | Role in the story |
|---|---|
| Town of Beginnings (fountain, Smith, Merchant, Skill Shrine, Hall of Clearers, waystone) | Home. Where the party forms, and where names are carved. |
| The fields and Wolf Woods | Where people level, and where the newcomers hid for forty days. |
| The raid gate and the Warden's arena | Floor 1's wall. |
| Mirelight Camp (Floor 2) | A lantern-lit refuge in the swamp. Night scenes use the Eclipse look. |
| The Drowned Wood (Floor 2 field) | Bog Lurkers under the mud; the Mire Matron. |
| The Sunken Gate and the Drowned Sovereign's court | Floor 2's wall. |

## The cast

| Name | Class (style) | Start Lv | Who they are | Want → Need |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Corvin** | Swordsman (one-handed sword) | 13 | Led the second raid on Day 30 and called the retreat too late. He's what's left of it. Clipped, focused, carries everyone. | The bell → to let someone else make the call. |

**Recurring words**
- *"Stay behind my shield. Every second."* / *"Every second."* (Bram → Pell; Ep 1, echoed by Tamsin)
- *"Then I come to you."* (Bram's promise; Pell keeps it in Ep 2)

## Episode beats

### Ep 1: First Line (full screenplay: `ep1-first-line.md`)
1. **Town, Hall of Clearers, day.** The Floor 1 stone is blank. Corvin and Bram. Tamsin and Juno join from the Smith. Pell runs in, Lv 8, begs to come. Bram: *"Stay behind my shield. Every second."*
2. **Raid gate.** Ready check, 5-second countdown. *"When it spins its horns, you get behind me." / "And if I can't?" / "Then I come to you."*
3. **Warden's arena, Eclipse.** Live fight through three phases. Phase 3: the Horn Sweep turns on Pell. Bram takes it with Shield Wall and falls. Pell kneels by him. Corvin Breaks the Warden at 10%, *"Switch!"*; Pell lands the Last Attack.
4. **Town, day.** The stone has one line: five names. **The first bell tolls across the tower.** Pell picks up Bram's Buckle. *"Floor two. I'll take the front."*

### Ep 2: Mirelight
1. **Mirelight Camp, night (Eclipse).** Tamsin sits writing the Count. Pell at the Skill Shrine wearing the Buckle. The **Ashen Hand** arrive: Sable, Dax and two of her own. Sable claims the Sunken Gate: *"We go first. Stay out of our way."* Juno: *"You bought every potion in town."* Sable: *"And I'll carry them home."*
2. **The Drowned Wood.** Both parties farm apart. Dax falls behind. The **Mire Matron**'s Call of the Mire: lurkers erupt under him. Sable's party is too far. Pell runs back alone: *"Then I come to you."* Our four kill the Matron; Dax lives.
3. **Camp.** Sable faces Corvin: *"You didn't have to."* Corvin: *"Nobody gets left at the stop."* Tamsin hesitates, then hands Dax a potion. Sable: *"Day after tomorrow. The gate. Together."*

### Ep 3: The Second Bell
1. **The Sunken Gate.** Six ready up: Corvin, Tamsin, Juno, Pell, Sable, Dax. Sable: *"Last Attack's mine." / Corvin: "Then earn it."*
2. **The Sovereign's court.** Live fight. Phase 2: the Tidal Wave, *"Roll through it!"*. Phase 3: the Deluge; Juno, in the dark, holds the bright ring and calls it: *"Inside the light! On me!"* At 10% Sable Breaks the Sovereign, looks at Tamsin: *"Switch! Healer, it's yours!"* Tamsin's Smite lands the Last Attack.
3. **Hall of Clearers, Floor 2 face.** Six names. **The second bell.** Tamsin closes the notebook: *"No new names."* Corvin: *"Ninety-eight to go."* Title card.

## What the story needs from the game
Every item is a task in `docs/ROADMAP.md` (section F, "Season 1: filming"), with a spec and acceptance criteria.
- **F1 Speech bubbles** above heads for chat lines.
- **F2 Scene director**: plays a cue sheet (YAML) with bot actors; admin page to load, run, stop.
- **F3 Staging hooks**: forced boss moves, reserved Last Attack, scripted death with no respawn, twists off, spawn a mob on cue, give a keepsake item.
- **F4 Cinematic camera**: shots (close-up, wide, over-the-shoulder, orbit), slow motion, letterbox, title cards, fades, a clean feed with no HUD, per-scene light (day / Eclipse).
- **F5 Poses**: kneel, sit, head bowed (procedural, on the KayKit rigs).
- **F6 The Tower Bell**: a real game feature. The server's first clear of a floor rings a bell for everyone, on every floor.
