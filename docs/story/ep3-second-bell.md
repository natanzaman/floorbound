# Episode 3: THE SECOND BELL
*Floorbound, Season 1 · about 4 minutes · 6 actors · cue sheet: to write in F7*

| Name | Class (style) | Lv | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| CORVIN | Swordsman (sword) | 17 | Learns to let someone else make the call. |
| TAMSIN | Cleric (staff) | 16 | Twenty-three names in the Count. |
| JUNO | Ranger (crossbow) | 16 | Finds her nerve in the dark. |
| PELL | Lancer (spear) | 16 | In front now. |
| SABLE | Berserker (twin axes) | 17 | Wanted the Last Attack. |
| DAX | Bard (war drum) | 16 | Keeps the beat. |

---

### 1. EXT. THE SUNKEN GATE — DAY  *(~40 s)*

FADE IN. Title card: **DAY 47**.

Six at the gate. They ready up `[F2 ready]`.

**SABLE:** Last Attack's mine. The Tidecloak's worth a floor.

**CORVIN:** Then earn it.

**DAX:** Drum's tuned. Songs on the Switch.

**TAMSIN:** Potions in the first ten seconds. Not the last.

**PELL:** Front's mine. Juno, you call the waves.

**JUNO:** …Sure. Love that for me.

The gate opens. FADE OUT.

---

### 2. INT. THE DROWNED SOVEREIGN'S COURT  *(~150 s, live)*

FADE IN. THE DROWNED SOVEREIGN rises from the water `[F4 crane]`.

**CORVIN:** Spread out! Geysers come up under you!

**Live fight, phase 1**, until below 66%.

The court trembles. The water draws back `[F3 forced Tidal Wave]`.

**JUNO:** Wave! Roll through it, don't run!

They roll through the wall of water `[F4 slow motion]`. **Live fight, phase 2**, Drowned Thralls and mire pools, until below 33%.

Phase 3: **the Deluge.** The outer court floods; the light shrinks to one bright ring `[F2 light: eclipse]`.

JUNO stops. Dark water everywhere. Her breath shakes.

**JUNO:** Inside the light! On me! Everyone on me!

They gather on her. **Live fight** until below 10%.

SABLE Breaks the Sovereign. She has the Switch; the Tidecloak is hers to take. She looks at TAMSIN.

**SABLE:** Switch! Healer, it's yours!

**TAMSIN:** Me?

**SABLE:** You've kept us all alive. End it!

TAMSIN's Smite lands: the Last Attack `[F3 reserved for Tamsin; F4 slow motion]`. The Sovereign sinks.

Silence. Then DAX, on the drum, one slow beat. JUNO laughs, shaky. Everyone Cheers.

FADE OUT.

---

### 3. EXT. MIRELIGHT CAMP, THE STONE — DAWN  *(~45 s)*

FADE IN. The camp's stone of clearers. Six names on the Floor 2 face `[F6 engraved]`.

**PELL:** Six names. Nobody missing.

TAMSIN opens the Count. She looks at it a long time. She closes it.

**TAMSIN:** No new names.

**The second bell** rolls down through the tower `[F6]`. They look up.

**SABLE:** That one's ours. All of ours.

**CORVIN:** Ninety-eight to go.

**PELL:** Front's mine.

**JUNO:** You keep saying that.

They walk to the well together `[F4 wide, crane]`.

Title card: **FLOOR 2 CLEARED** / *ninety-eight bells to go* · **END OF SEASON 1**. FADE OUT.
