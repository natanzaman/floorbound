# Episode 1: FIRST LINE
*Floorbound, Season 1 · about 3½ minutes · 5 actors · cue sheet: `ep1-first-line.yaml`*

| Name | Class (style) | Lv | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| CORVIN | Swordsman (sword) | 13 | Raid leader; survived the second raid. |
| BRAM | Guardian (sword & shield) | 12 | The wall. Calm, few words. |
| TAMSIN | Cleric (staff) | 11 | Keeps the Count. |
| JUNO | Ranger (crossbow) | 11 | Dry humour. |
| PELL | Lancer (spear) | 8 | Underleveled newcomer. |

Tags in brackets name the game feature a beat uses (ROADMAP section F).

---

### 1. EXT. TOWN OF BEGINNINGS, HALL OF CLEARERS — DAY  *(~45 s)*

FADE IN on letterbox. Title card: **DAY 41** `[F4]`

The Hall of Clearers. Its Floor 1 face is blank. CORVIN stands before it. `[F4 close-up on the stone, then wide]`

**CORVIN:** Forty-one days. Not one name on this stone.

BRAM walks up beside him and faces the stone.

**BRAM:** Three raids tried.

**CORVIN:** I'm what's left of the second.

**BRAM:** Then we go in smarter. All of us.

TAMSIN and JUNO walk over from the Smith.

**JUNO:** Bolts are fresh. Tamsin's carrying forty potions.

**TAMSIN:** Thirty-eight. Juno keeps "testing" them.

PELL runs in from the square.

**PELL:** Wait! I heard you need a fifth.

**JUNO:** You're level eight.

**PELL:** I'm fast. I don't freeze. Please.

CORVIN looks at BRAM. BRAM faces PELL.

**BRAM:** Stay behind my shield. Every second. Understood?

**PELL:** Every second.

**CORVIN:** Then we're five. Raid gate.

They walk off toward the north road. FADE OUT.

---

### 2. EXT. THE RAID GATE — DAY  *(~30 s)*

FADE IN. The party at the portcullis. PELL steps in behind BRAM.

**BRAM:** When it spins its horns, you get behind me.

**PELL:** And if I can't?

**BRAM:** Then I come to you.

**CORVIN:** Nobody dies today. That's the only order.

They ready up. The 5-second countdown starts. `[F2 ready]`

**TAMSIN:** Five seconds. Last chance to turn back.

Nobody moves. The gate rises. They walk through. FADE OUT.

---

### 3. INT. THE WARDEN'S ARENA — ECLIPSE  *(~100–120 s, live)*

FADE IN. Dark sky, dimmed arena. THE HORNED WARDEN at the centre. No twist this time `[F3 twists off]`; its Last Attack is reserved for Pell `[F3]`.

**BRAM:** Taunt's up! Hit it from the sides!

BRAM taunts. **Live fight, phase 1**, until the Warden is below 66%.

The hounds pour in.

**JUNO:** Hounds! I'll keep them off Tamsin!

**TAMSIN:** Sanctuary down! Stay inside the light!

**Live fight, phase 2**, until below 33%.

Phase 3. The Warden coils, horns low, and turns on PELL `[F3 forced Horn Sweep]`.

**CORVIN:** Pell! Horn Sweep!

BRAM runs to PELL and raises Shield Wall. The sweep lands on him `[F4 close-up, slow motion]`.

**BRAM:** Told you. I come to you.

BRAM collapses `[F3 scripted death: stays down, no respawn]`.

**PELL:** Bram!

PELL kneels by him `[F5 kneel]`.

**TAMSIN:** His bar's empty. He's… he's gone.

**CORVIN:** Pell. Up. We finish it. For him.

PELL stands. **Live fight** until the Warden is below 10%.

CORVIN Breaks the Warden.

**CORVIN:** Switch!

PELL runs in and drives his spear home: the Last Attack `[F4 slow motion]`. The Warden falls.

JUNO throws her arms up in a Cheer, then lowers them.

CUT TO: the Town of Beginnings, empty. The old well in the square. **A bright light emits from the well**: it starts to glow, and keeps glowing. The way up is open `[F4 wide; the well wakes]`.

CUT BACK TO: the arena. PELL walks to where BRAM fell and kneels. He picks up **Bram's Buckle** `[F3 keepsake item]`.

**PELL:** He told me to stay behind him.

**TAMSIN:** You did. Every second.

FADE OUT.

---

### 4. EXT. TOWN OF BEGINNINGS, HALL OF CLEARERS — DAY  *(~35 s)*

FADE IN. The four survivors walk up to the stone. The Floor 1 face now has one line: five names `[F4 close-up on the stone]`.

**JUNO:** Five names. It kept his.

**CORVIN:** First line on the stone. Where he always stood.

TAMSIN bows her head `[F5 bow]`.

Then, far above, **a bell**. Once, deep, long. Every player in the tower stops and looks up `[F6 the Tower Bell]`.

**TAMSIN:** The first bell.

**PELL:** Floor two. I'll take the front.

They walk to the well, glowing now: the way up `[F4 wide, crane up]`.

Title card: **FLOOR 1 CLEARED** / *ninety-nine bells to go* `[F4]`. FADE OUT.

*END OF EPISODE 1.*
