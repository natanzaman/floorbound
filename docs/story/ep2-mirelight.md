# Episode 2: MIRELIGHT
*Floorbound, Season 1 · about 3½ minutes · 6 actors · cue sheet: to write in F7*

| Name | Class (style) | Lv | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| CORVIN | Swordsman (sword) | 15 | Leads, still carrying everyone. |
| TAMSIN | Cleric (staff) | 14 | The Count has one more name. |
| JUNO | Ranger (crossbow) | 14 | Hates the dark; the camp is dark. |
| PELL | Lancer (spear) | 13 | Wears Bram's Buckle. |
| SABLE | Berserker (twin axes) | 16 | Leader of the Ashen Hand. |
| DAX | Bard (war drum) | 15 | Sable's drummer. |

---

### 1. EXT. MIRELIGHT CAMP — NIGHT (Eclipse)  *(~70 s)*

FADE IN. Title card: **DAY 44 · THE GLIMMERING MIRE**.

Lanterns over black water. TAMSIN sits by the tents `[F5 sit]`. PELL stands at the Skill Shrine. JUNO keeps close to the light.

**JUNO:** I hate this floor. Even the water glows wrong.

**CORVIN:** Sunken Gate's past the Drowned Wood. Two days, if we level.

SABLE and DAX step out of the glow at the camp fire (the camp's waypoint: they came up through the well). SABLE's twin axes are on her back; DAX's drum on his arm.

**SABLE:** You're the ones who rang the bell.

**CORVIN:** We are.

**SABLE:** Good. Then you've had yours. The Sunken Gate is ours.

**JUNO:** You bought every potion in town.

**SABLE:** And I'll carry them home. Stay out of our way.

She walks off toward the Drowned Wood. DAX lingers.

**DAX:** Sorry. She's… it's been a long forty days.

**PELL:** Forty-four.

DAX nods and follows her. TAMSIN closes her notebook. FADE OUT.

---

### 2. EXT. THE DROWNED WOOD — DAY  *(~80 s, live)*

FADE IN. Dead trees, mist, rings of bubbling mud.

Our four fight Bog Lurkers (**live**) while, across the clearing, SABLE and DAX fight their own. DAX falls behind, playing to the trees.

**JUNO:** Their drummer's drifting.

The MIRE MATRON rises behind DAX `[F3 spawn]`. Her **Call of the Mire**: lurkers erupt all around him.

**DAX:** Sable! SABLE!

SABLE is too far out, surrounded.

**SABLE:** Hold on! I can't—

PELL is already running `[F4 follow shot]`.

**CORVIN:** Pell! That's not our fight!

**PELL:** Then I come to you!

PELL reaches DAX and puts himself between him and the Matron. CORVIN swears, and the party follows.

**CORVIN:** Switch on the Matron! Juno, the lurkers!

**Live fight** until the Matron is dead. DAX lives.

DAX, out of breath, faces PELL.

**DAX:** Why?

PELL touches the Buckle `[F5 bow: head down]`.

**PELL:** Someone did it for me.

FADE OUT.

---

### 3. EXT. MIRELIGHT CAMP — NIGHT (Eclipse)  *(~45 s)*

FADE IN. SABLE walks up to CORVIN.

**SABLE:** You didn't have to.

**CORVIN:** Nobody gets left at the stop.

**SABLE:** What's that mean?

**CORVIN:** It was his rule. The man we lost.

A long beat. TAMSIN stands, walks to DAX, and hands him a potion `[F2 use_item / give]`.

**TAMSIN:** You're out. I can see your bar.

**DAX:** …Thanks.

**SABLE:** Day after tomorrow. The Sunken Gate. Together.

**CORVIN:** Together.

JUNO, under the lanterns, lets out a breath.

**JUNO:** Great. More people to be scared with.

Title card: **NEXT: THE SECOND BELL**. FADE OUT.
